# Gitcko Corporate Contributor License Agreement

> **DRAFT, not yet in force.** This text is waiting for legal review. Its structure follows the Apache Software Foundation's Corporate CLA. Until the reviewed version is published, contributions can't be merged under it.

This agreement is between the company or organisation named below (the "Corporation") and Anthony Jabbour, or any successor entity he assigns this agreement to (the "Licensor"). It lets the Corporation's employees and contractors listed in Schedule A ("Designated Employees") contribute to the `anthony-jabbour/gitcko` repository (the "Project") on the Corporation's behalf.

## 1. Definitions

- **"Contribution"**: any original work of authorship, including changes or additions to existing work, that a Designated Employee intentionally submits to the Project on the Corporation's behalf, through a pull request, an issue, a patch or any other means. Anything clearly marked as "Not a Contribution" when it is submitted is excluded.

## 3. Patent licence

The Corporation grants the Licensor, and anyone who receives software from the Licensor, a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, no-charge, royalty-free, irrevocable patent licence to make, have made, use, offer to sell, sell, import and otherwise transfer its Contributions and the Project. It covers only the patent claims the Corporation can license that are necessarily infringed by a Contribution alone, or by a Contribution combined with the Project as it was when the Contribution was submitted. If anyone starts patent litigation claiming that a Contribution or the Project infringes a patent, the patent licences the Corporation granted to that party under this agreement end on the date the litigation is filed.

## 4. The Corporation's promises

- The Corporation is legally entitled to grant these licences, and the person signing is authorised to sign for it.
- Each Contribution is the Corporation's original work, or the Corporation has the right to submit it under this agreement. If a Contribution includes someone else's work, the submitter says so: its source, its licence, and any restrictions the Corporation knows of.
- The Corporation keeps Schedule A up to date and tells the Licensor when someone should be added or removed.

## 5. The Licensor's promise

Every Contribution that is merged into the public Project repository stays available there under the GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 or later, or a more permissive open-source licence.

## 6. No warranty

The Corporation provides its Contributions "as is", without warranties or conditions of any kind, unless it agrees otherwise in writing. It doesn't have to provide support for them.
