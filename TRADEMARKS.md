# Gitcko trademark policy

"Gitcko" and the Gitcko logo are trademarks of Anthony Jabbour (the "licensor"). Gitcko's code licences (AGPL-3.0-or-later and Apache-2.0) cover the code only. They don't grant any rights to the name or the logo.

This policy follows the approach of the Rust and Mozilla trademark policies: use the name freely to talk about Gitcko, but don't make people think something is Gitcko when it isn't.

## You may, without asking

- Say that your product, plugin or service "works with Gitcko", "supports Gitcko" or "integrates with Gitcko", as long as it's true.
- Link to Gitcko and refer to it by name in articles, talks, books, videos and teaching material.
- Redistribute **unmodified** Gitcko builds under the Gitcko name.
- Use the name and logo for community events and user groups about Gitcko, if it's clear they aren't run by the licensor.

## You may not, without written permission

- Name a fork, a modified build, a hosted service or another product "Gitcko", or anything confusingly similar.
- Use the Gitcko logo for a modified build or for your own product.
- Suggest that the licensor endorses, sponsors or runs your product or service.
- Register a domain name, package name or social media account that could be mistaken for an official Gitcko one.

## Forks

Forks are welcome under the code licences. A fork must use a different name and remove the Gitcko logo before it is distributed or offered as a service. Saying "based on Gitcko" is fine.

Open a GitHub issue or contact the maintainers listed in [MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md). If you're unsure whether a use is allowed, ask first.
